## New: Quick Start
F1 > Enhancements > General > Start-up > **Quick Start** (off by default). With it on, the next launch goes straight into town: the screen stays black for a few seconds and the first thing you see is your resident stepping out of the house. Pick who you play as under **Play as** if your town has more than one resident.

- **Your save is handled exactly as in a normal start.** The game's own start-up still runs (title, Rover, resident select, "Preparing saved data"), just hidden and as fast as your PC allows, so every save check and write happens as usual. Mr. Resetti, letters, events and the new day all still happen in town.
- **About 5 seconds** from opening the game to walking out of the house, instead of about a minute (with the shaders already prepared; the first launch after an update takes longer).
- **Normal start for one launch:** hold Shift (or B) while the game starts.
- **It steps aside by itself** when there is no save yet, the save can't be read, the chosen resident has moved out, or Rover says something unusual; you then get the normal screens. F1 shows what the last launch did and why.
