## Fixes
- **Hats reflect on your head.** With a hat on, the reflection in the water showed loose pieces of it floating past the head (the wizard's cap as scattered stars). Hats, glasses, masks, umbrellas, tools and anything held up are now mirrored together with the character, villagers' umbrellas in the rain included.
- **The fishing rod reflects once.** Fishing, the rod showed up in the water as a broken stack of several pieces. It is now one rod in the reflection; the bobber is unchanged.
- **Menus no longer jump at high frame rates.** Above 30 fps the scrolling pattern behind menu windows jumped back every so often. It now scrolls smoothly.
- **The pointer goes to OK on pop-ups.** When a message such as "You can't place an item here" opens over a menu, the pointer now snaps to its OK button instead of the menu items underneath.

Nothing changes with water reflections off. The reflection fix costs no measurable GPU time.
